**Subject:** Getting started with MemberPerkClub — 4 quick steps

Hi [First name],

Great speaking with you today. Here's everything you need to get set up. The
whole process takes about five minutes, and there's no cost until you enroll
your first client.

**How it works, in short:** your producer account is free, with no contract
and no monthly fee. You buy memberships at the wholesale rate of **$12 each**,
and you decide what your client pays, up to the $149 public price. You keep
the difference. There's no commission to wait on, because the margin is
already yours. Each membership lasts one year, is a one-time charge, and
never auto-renews.

**Step 1 — Create your account**
Go to https://memberperkclub.com/producer-signup and fill in your name,
business name, email, phone and business address. Your business name is what
your clients will see in their member dashboard all year, so use the name
they know you by.

**Step 2 — Activate your account**
Right after you sign up, we'll email you a sign-in link from
club@memberperkclub.com. Click **Sign in** and you're in. There's no password
to remember: whenever you want to sign in again, go to
https://memberperkclub.com/login, choose **Email me a sign-in link**, and
we'll send you a fresh one. If it doesn't show up within a couple of minutes,
check your spam folder.

**Step 3 — Add a payment method (do this first)**
You can't enroll clients until this is done, and you only do it once. Go to
https://memberperkclub.com/producer/payment-method and add a card. Your card
is stored securely by Stripe, our payment processor — we never see the
number. It's charged $12 only at the moment you choose to enroll a client,
never automatically and never on a schedule.

**Step 4 — Enroll your first client**
Go to https://memberperkclub.com/producer/enroll, enter your client's name,
email, phone and state, and click **Enroll client — charge $12**. Their
membership is active immediately, for one year.

**What happens next**
Your client will receive a welcome email with their member number and a link
to set their own password. Once they've done that, they can sign in and start
using their benefits right away. You'll also get an email confirming the
enrollment.

You can see every client you've enrolled at
https://memberperkclub.com/producer/dashboard. If a client says their welcome
email never arrived, ask them to check spam first — and you can resend it
from your dashboard with one click.

A couple of things worth knowing: memberships are non-refundable once
purchased, and your clients never see the $12 price — only what you choose to
charge them.

Any questions, just reply to this email.
